# How the AI Works

This page explains how Borderless AI calculates visa scores and why you can trust the results.

---

## The Short Version

The AI **does not make up scores.** Visa points are always calculated using fixed, real government rules — the same ones immigration officers use. The AI's job is only to **explain** the results in simple language.

---

## Step-by-Step: What Happens When You Submit the Form

### Step 1 — We Clean Up Your Input
When you fill out the form, we convert your answers into a standard format. For example:
- "Full stack developer" → "Software Engineer"
- "IELTS 7" → "English Level C1"
- "5 years experience" → "Senior level"

This makes sure your answers can be compared fairly against visa rules.

### Step 2 — We Check the Rules
We have the real visa requirements for each country hardcoded in the system. For example, Canada's Express Entry has a points table — your age, education, experience, and language scores each earn specific points. We calculate this mathematically, no AI guessing involved.

### Step 3 — We Check for Hard Blockers
Some requirements are absolute. If you fail one, you're blocked — no matter how good the rest of your profile is.

**Example:** The US H-1B visa requires an employer to sponsor you. If you don't have a job offer, you're blocked. A high English score won't help.

### Step 4 — We Search for Real Sources
We search a database of 1,454+ official government documents to find the most relevant rules, exceptions, and current processing times for your situation.

### Step 5 — The AI Reads and Explains
The AI reads the official documents and your calculation results, then writes a plain-English explanation of:
- Why you got this score
- What's holding you back
- What you can do to improve

The AI **cannot change your score.** It can only explain it.

### Step 6 — You See Your Results
You get a ranked list of your best visa options, each with:
- A score (0–100)
- What requirements you meet ✅ and what's missing ❌
- How to improve your score
- Links to official government pages

## Why This Is More Reliable Than Just Using ChatGPT

| Problem with plain ChatGPT | How Borderless AI solves it |
|---|---|
| Makes up visa rules | We use hardcoded, fixed rules from real governments |
| Has outdated information | We search a live database of official documents |
| Gives yes/no answers | We give exact scores and explain every point |
| No sources | Every answer is linked to official government pages |
| Overconfident | We show confidence levels and flag uncertain areas |

## The What-If Simulator

On your results page, you can adjust your inputs (like your English score or savings) and see how your score changes **instantly** — no need to resubmit the form.

This works because the scoring rules run directly in your browser. When you move a slider, the math recalculates in milliseconds.

**Example:** "If I improve my IELTS from 7 to 8, I'd gain +15 points in Canada Express Entry and move from 68 → 83."

## Honesty & Limitations

- We always show a confidence level with each result
- If we're not sure about something, we say "Needs Verification"
- Scores are estimates — real immigration decisions depend on many factors
- Always verify with a licensed immigration lawyer before applying
